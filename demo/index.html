The video that waits for the learner.

This page runs createGatedPlayer(), the same controller that drives <GatedPlayer> on Fire TV (Vega OS), on a plain HTML <video>. The video pauses at each .istep gate. A simulated learner streams pose and hand landmarks, and the gate engine decides when the video may go on.

      How to try it. Press Start lesson with the learner on Watch. The video pauses at the first gate and stays paused. Switch to Perform and the gate engine passes the gate, so the video goes on. In a real app the landmarks come from a pose tracker (InStep uses MediaPipe in the browser of a phone). This page has no camera and sends nothing over the network.

      Lesson videos and .istep tracks: InStep demonstration lessons, illustrated animations with no real person, CC-BY-4.0. The fingerspelling handshapes are illustrated and not yet reviewed by Deaf signers. Narration: synthetic Kokoro-82M voice. Details in InStep's CREDITS.md.
